# Mission: Trade & invest on TraderMercury's principles

## Why
Build durable wealth as a **long-term investor + short-term swing trader** while holding a
full-time job — so trading fits into ~2 focused sessions a week, not a screen-staring habit.
Internalise TraderMercury's philosophy so decisions are your own (no borrowed conviction),
then hand the *codified* rules to an AI + MCP system that does the heavy lifting.

## Success looks like
- Glance at any chart and classify its structure + higher-timeframe context in seconds.
- Run a fast bi-weekly watchlist scan and pick only strong, momentum assets worth triggers.
- Have a written entry/invalidation/exit plan per position before risking money.
- One compressed lesson + reference per TraderMercury video, principles distilled.
- Eventually: AI reads a chart against these codified rules and proposes buy decisions.
- Eventually: a live system — watchlist + entry/exit plans + triggers — in Notion & TradingView via MCP.

## Constraints
- Working professional. Very little time. Reviews trades ~bi-weekly.
- Learning is transcript-driven: TraderMercury videos are the primary source.
- Must stay simple (his core creed) — no indicator soup.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Day trading / intraday scalping.
- Options, leverage mechanics, funding rates, RSI/volume indicators.
- Building the automated MCP system before the principles are solid.
