Price discovery is where the wealth is

"This is actually how you're probably going to make most of your wealth." In price discovery nobody knows fair value: the market is literally discovering it (SOL $1→$250, DOGE 0.2¢→75¢). Balance being a moonboy with "gravity does exist".

The fib-extension ladder

Pick a relevant high → low (arbitrary, but justify why). The golden pocket (0.618–0.65) is the last resistance before discovery. Above it, extensions are the targets: 1.618 → 2.618 → 3.618 → 4.236 (then 6.85, 11.09). He only uses fibs in price discovery. They're a "magic number I just made up", but they are self-fulfilling: in uncharted territory everyone draws the same ones.

Take profit, ask questions later

"You cannot profit from markets without taking profit." Sell some at each extension, then ask: rejection? Pullback buy? Local-trend add? At a big resistance with the trend intact, "play it as such": "resistance is resistance… the trend is the trend", so take part and hold the rest.

Exit on the trend break, not the target

Moonboy is fine while the 4H 200s + daily structure hold. Only the y-axis changes: the argument is identical at $2.40, $12.60, $20 and $38. Lose the 4H 200s, print a daily LL then an LH, lose demand, and "even a four-year-old knows this time is different". Stop targeting the next fib.

Be confident early, not late

Conviction belongs at the 200D reclaim + key trend shift + first weekly structure break in 8 months after a −60% downtrend. Late-confidence people "didn't see it coming". Be both: moonboy and knife-dodger

Moonboys held SOL $3→$250→back down because they targeted 10k or "flip BTC". Downside-navigators missed the rally. Logarithmic thinkers are usually diamond hands with no sell plan ("million or back to zero"). Be both. "I am bullish at the exact Pico top" is fine: the local shift tests the HTF trend (−12%), the 4H 200s loss → chop → breakdown, and the −60% is avoided.

The retire-this-cycle loop

Same philosophy every cycle: a new fib from the cycle high → low, targets 1.618…4.236. "Yes, I am an absolute moon boy." Lose the 4H 200s → you can't justify the fib, so wait for a reclaim (run it back, same argument, which may fail). If 11.09 cracks, redraw from the new high → low. "We're never reliant on the market, we're reliant on a thought process."
