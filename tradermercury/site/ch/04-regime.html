Regime: trend vs range vs chop; fair value

After this: classify any chart as trend, range or chop in one look, and switch tools to match.

Green, red, orange

He circles periods by regime: green is up, red is down, orange is sideways. "The way you screw up in markets is by trading these red periods as if they're green", or by treating orange as if it's about to turn. A trend is a persistence assumption: if it's heading top right, assume it keeps going top right until it stops.

Match the strategy to the environment

Don't mean-revert in a strong trend, don't buy dips in a downtrend, and don't run a trend system in a market that isn't trending. "Think big enough during expanding periods" and not too big in contraction.

The "magic trick": MAs draw the circles for you

Count your trends

Count how often your bias flips. One bias for ~35 days over 40% of price is a trend. Four bias shifts in a week over ~8–10% means the environment changed. "10 days, one outlook; three days, three different outlooks: maybe you're just getting chopped." Once you're at three flips, stop doing trend analysis and draw a range. "Fool me three times, we're probably not trending anymore."

A weak reclaim is a tell

After a −30% week, a trend reclaim that gives only +5% ("abysmal") and then breaks back down makes that high a deviation. A reclaim should resume the trend by a directionally proportional amount. If it doesn't, expect chop.

Range mindset, or step away

Nothing significant happens inside a range. He stopped caring about the 15m 200s in a ~5% range, and only the break matters. "Confirmation" just means higher probabilities, nothing is ever confirmed. In chop, "take two steps back". If the range breaks down, use "vacation mode": take weeks off and come back to buy 20–40% lower.

Range = fair value being built

A long range is "value being built, it's fuel, the market deciding that this is a fair price". Anchor a VWAP where the range starts and it traces fair value. The longer price spends there, the bigger the move when it leaves: at least 30–50%. Repeated tests of the range lows aren't strength.

Expansion = repricing. Don't fight it.

A break out of fair value means the market has decided price was mis-valued. "Eventually this sideways will expand… you don't want to fight it." No revenge-shorting the leg, no fading it. His history: a ~2-month range led to an exaggerated leg up, a ~3-month distribution led to −50%, and the 2018 bottom (~130 days at ~3.8k) led to ~+230%.

If → then

  • Bias flips ≥3 times in a few days → stop trend analysis, draw the range.
  • Trend reclaim gives a measly move and fails → it was a deviation. Expect the old trend to resume.
  • Inside a range → ignore LTF MAs. Act only on the break.
  • A long time at fair value (AVWAP from the range start) then a break → don't fight it. Expect 30–50%+.
  • A major range breakdown → vacation mode. Rebid much lower.
  • Range lows tested for the 5th+ time → overcrowded. Wait for LTF strength first.
