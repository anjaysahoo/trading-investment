The bi-weekly ritual checklist

After this: run the whole system in one sitting every two weeks, leave limit orders behind, and touch nothing until the next review.

The ritual on one page

Every step is a filter. Most review days end at one of the "do nothing" exits, and that's the system working. Buttons only get pressed at the end, and only as pre-placed limit orders. His "99% of the time is waiting" is built in.
