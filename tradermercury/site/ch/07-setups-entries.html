Two ways in: bid the zone (A) or wait for the reclaim (B)

At a HTF zone you can step in front of the train and bid it (A), or wait for the LTF breakout and 30m 200s reclaim, then "assume the low is in" (B). "Everything is a balancing act between probabilities and risk reward." They move inversely, like a seesaw.

Don't pre-bid a slow bleed

The fear isn't missing the bottom. "Your fear is I get my entry and then some": the bid fills and price keeps bleeding. A fast impulse into a zone can be pre-bid. A slow grind with a dying narrative can't. Set-and-forget bids only work in the right environment. Otherwise wait for LTF strength. "You're obviously much better off waiting for some strength to develop after having tested a key region."

Missing is fine. Chasing isn't.

If the trend MA bounces before your zone, the bid was front-ran: no fill, keep watching. If LTF strength shows before your retest, cancel the bids and accept the opportunity cost. No market orders into illiquid breakouts (slippage). Late catch-up entries are "musical chairs": in thin markets they get stopped.

If → then

  • Zone has MA + S/R + structure at one price → valid setup. One part alone → paper foundation, skip.
  • Fast impulse into a stacked HTF zone → bid it (A).
  • Slow bleed, illiquid, dying narrative → don't pre-bid. Wait for strength or skip.
  • Price turned before your bid → front-ran. No chase.
  • Zone lost at review → cancel the bid. That's the plan working.
  • Range broken in the HTF trend direction → bid the old range high + HTF 200s.
