## Investor v/s Trader

### _When you are **TRADER** don't think about **TIME**._

A trade might take months to playout, or it can playout in next 1 day also. Only thing matter whether what you decided playout or not

A 3-year time horizon can also be considered as swing long trade

### _But when you are **INVESTOR**, **TIME** matters most_

You can't tell what will be price doing in next 6 months but what you can tell, 5 years from today it will be higher than current value and higher in 10 years. Because when you invest you are subscribing to fundamental of that asset not what's its value is now. 

If you are not confident of fundamental of a asset then you will juggle between trader and investor for an asset, and you will definitely lose money

Anything less than 5 years should not be considered as investment
