Market Structure — Cheat Sheet

The Rule of Fours. Print this. It is the whole foundation.

The four states

StateHighs & lowsShapeTrade it?
UptrendHigher highs, higher lowsStairs up ↗Yes — momentum longs
DowntrendLower highs, lower lowsStairs down ↘No (long-biased) — watch for reversal only
ConsolidationLower highs BUT higher lowsTriangle ▷Wait — coiling, no direction yet
RangeEqual highs, equal lowsSideways ▭Wait — chop, low momentum

Reading routine

  1. 4-second glance. Up, down, or sideways? A four-year-old could say.
  2. Name the swings. HH/HL or LH/LL? If unsure, switch to a line chart to see the wave.
  3. Zoom out for context. What is the higher timeframe doing? It trumps the lower one.
  4. Decide. Strong uptrend with friendly context → watchlist. Chop/downtrend → discard.

Rules of thumb

  • Strength attracts strength. Buy assets already trending up, not laggards hoping to turn.
  • Higher timeframe wins. A low-timeframe uptrend inside a higher-timeframe downtrend is a trap.
  • Structure is interpretive. Two honest traders can disagree on the same chart. Capture the gist, don't split textbook hairs.
  • No indicator soup. Structure first. RSI/volume/funding are noise at this layer.
