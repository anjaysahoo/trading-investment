# TraderMercury Trading Glossary

The canonical language for this workspace. Terms distilled from TraderMercury's videos.

## Market Structure

**Market structure**:
The sequence of highs and lows that reveals what the market is doing. The most objective
read of an asset's direction.

**Higher high / higher low (HH/HL)**:
Each swing peak and trough sits above the last. The signature of an **uptrend**.

**Lower high / lower low (LH/LL)**:
Each swing peak and trough sits below the last. The signature of a **downtrend**.

**Rule of Fours**:
The market can only ever do one of four things: uptrend, downtrend, consolidation, or range.
Every analysis stems from one of these.

**Consolidation**:
Lower highs *but* higher lows — price squeezing into a triangle. A coil, not a direction.

**Range**:
Relatively equal highs and equal lows — price ping-ponging sideways between two bounds.

**4-second rule**:
Even a four-year-old can name a chart's direction within four seconds. If it takes longer,
you are overcomplicating it.

**Context (multi-timeframe)**:
The same chart can be an uptrend on a low timeframe and a downtrend on a higher one. Always
read the higher timeframe first — it carries more weight and shifts less often.

## Philosophy

**Simplicity creed**:
"The height of cultivation is merely simplicity" (Bruce Lee). Tune out the noise — no RSI,
funding, open interest, or even volume. Read what the chart *is*, not *why*.

**Trade your analysis**:
Every trade must execute the analysis you actually made. Deviating means you're being
impulsive/emotional, not disciplined.

**Borrowed conviction**:
Holding a position on someone else's thesis you don't understand. You can't hold or
invalidate it under pressure — so "you cannot borrow conviction."

**Momentum trading**:
Prioritise strong, uptrending assets. Strength attracts strength. Buy strength, not hope.

**Long-biased**:
Trading only long — swing/spot positions targeting new all-time highs. Downtrends are
mostly ignored (only watched for a possible future reversal).
