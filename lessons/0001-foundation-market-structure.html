Read Any Chart in 4 Seconds

Market structure · Rule of Fours · momentum over prediction.

"The height of cultivation is merely simplicity — the utmost with the minimum." — Bruce Lee, via TraderMercury

Why (for you): ~2 sessions/week, no babysitting charts. The edge = a read so fast you act only on the strongest few.

1 · Execution > analysis

95% lose, yet all of them analyse. Market only goes up or down — analysis is just a bet on which. The gap is execution, and you can only execute what you understand: you cannot borrow conviction.

2 · The Rule of Fours

Four states. Every chart, every timeframe, is one of these. See the shape — don't memorise words.

4-second rule: a 4-year-old names up/down/sideways instantly. Hard to see the swings? Flip to a line chart — the wave pops.

3 · Context: higher timeframe wins

Same asset, two zooms. A low-timeframe uptrend can sit inside a higher-timeframe downtrend — a trap.

4 · Momentum: strength attracts strength

Trade strong assets already trending up, targeting new highs. Long-biased. Bi-weekly job: spacebar-scan TradingView, keep clean uptrends, bin the chop.

5 · Proof — Hyperliquid vs Bitcoin (Jan 27)

Same day. Hype broke a higher high on daily+weekly; BTC chopped near range lows in a higher-TF downtrend.

Same market, opposite outcomes — decided by reading structure and buying strength.

Skill check — classify the chart

Recall from memory. Pick one:

Q1. Peaks 100 → 108 → 115, dips 95 → 102. State?

Q2. Lower highs but higher lows, squeezing. State?

Q3. 4h up, weekly clearly down. Best move?

Takeaways

  • Rule of Fours: up · down · consolidation · range.
  • 4-second rule: hard read = overcomplicating.
  • Context first: higher timeframe trumps lower.
  • Buy strength: momentum longs on clean uptrends.
  • No borrowed conviction: trade only what you understand.
